## Primary-source research starting points

- [Odoo POS](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/point_of_sale.html): general workflow reference, not a requirement to add its restaurant features.
- [Odoo products](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/point_of_sale/products.html): variants, lot/serial concepts; serials remain excluded.
- [Odoo expiry](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/product_management/product_tracking/expiration_dates.html): batch/expiry traceability reference.
- [Odoo 19 FEFO removal](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/shipping_receiving/removal_strategies/fefo.html): current primary documentation confirms that lot assignment is required during receiving for tracked goods, FEFO chooses the soonest expiry/removal date first and can split demand across successive lots. MyPoS adopts the lot/FEFO mechanics but deliberately excludes serial tracking and manufacturing. Odoo describes an alert after an expiration date has passed; T015 therefore defines the deterministic application rule as sellable through the printed store-local date and blocked from the next local day. This boundary is a product decision inferred from that wording, not a claim about Pakistani law or a pharmaceutical certification.
- [Lightspeed purchasing](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/15256357228187-Managing-purchase-orders): purchase-order lifecycle reference.
- [Shopify POS device FAQ](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/faq): distinguish native POS from browser back office.
- [Square offline payments](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/7777-process-card-payments-with-offline-mode): provider/hardware-specific limitations; not evidence of Pakistan availability or our offline card support.
- [MDN storage quotas/eviction](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Storage_API/Storage_quotas_and_eviction_criteria): browser durability limits.
- [QZ signing](https://qz.io/docs/signing): evaluate helper security/offline feasibility; QZ is not yet a committed provider.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/): accessibility reference.
- [GS1 standards repository](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/): on 2026-09-29 the repository identified GS1 General Specifications 26.0.0, last modified 2026-01-27, as current.
- [GS1 General Specifications 26.0.0](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/genspecs/26.0.0/): variable-measure retail items may use restricted-circulation prefixes 02 and 20–29, while the remaining fields and whether they encode product, quantity or price are defined by the applicable national or internal scheme. T011 therefore uses bounded merchant/store configuration rather than assuming one Pakistan layout.
- [GS1 check-digit calculator](https://www.gs1.org/services/check-digit-calculator): fixed-length numeric GS1 identification keys use a final integrity check digit. T011 supports optional EAN-13 check-digit validation for configured scale labels and rejects a label when the check fails.
- [FBR digital invoicing assistance](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/di-technical-assistance/173967/173970): follow current official API documentation and legal applicability links.
- [SBP Raast merchant payments](https://www.sbp.org.pk/our-subsidiaries/raast/raast-person-to-merchant): future payment-provider research, not a launch acquisition integration.

Also inspect official Laravel, Vue, MySQL and chosen dependency documentation during T002. Record exact supporting links next to each recommendation. Competitor pricing or features must be dated and qualified by plan/country/hardware.
